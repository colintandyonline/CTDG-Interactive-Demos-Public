BEYOND A CHAT WIDGET / BUILT FOR YOUR BUSINESS

A receptionist designed around how your business actually operates.

CTDG can design the conversation, knowledge, escalation rules and authorised actions around a wide range of businesses. The right architecture depends on your industry, team, software, access permissions and compliance requirements — not a generic chatbot template.

Live conversations and business knowledge

Website chat and voice where supported, service-specific knowledge, intent recognition and relevant questions. Cora demonstrates the live CTDG conversation and speech experience here.

Qualified enquiries and verified actions

Capture the essentials with consent and prepare a reviewable brief. CTDG's own Cora already connects to our enquiry database and email pipeline; client-specific actions need their own authorised integrations.

CRM, booking and support handoffs

Potential integrations include CRM records, calendars, help desks, email and workflow tools. Each is assessed for available APIs, data access, business rules, testing and a reliable fallback before it can be activated.

Human oversight and escalation

Route sensitive, urgent, uncertain or high-value requests to the appropriate team. Never claim a booking, refund or follow-up was completed unless the connected system confirms it.

Protected business information

Limit access to the approved data, use appropriate privacy controls and maintain auditable handoffs. Highly regulated use cases require additional safeguards and may need restricted or human-led handling.

Support, monitoring and improvement

Depending on the agreed plan: usage and failure monitoring, knowledge reviews, escalation checks, maintenance, operational reporting and controlled changes as your business evolves.

Live here versus configurable for clients: CTDG's Cora can answer through the live AI service, generate speech and record consent-based CTDG enquiries. The customer-facing appointment, CRM, payment and help-desk examples are simulations until a client-specific integration has been configured, tested and approved.
WHY A MANAGED AI RECEPTIONIST IS DIFFERENT

More useful than an ordinary FAQ chatbot.

The real business value isn't just answering a question. It's understanding the request, safely supporting the next action and ensuring someone is accountable for what happens afterwards.

  • May leave staff to copy enquiries manually

CTDG / SCOPED MANAGED RECEPTION

Helps the business take action

We define a practical role for the receptionist within your actual operating workflow.

  • Business-specific knowledge and qualification questions
  • Approved CRM, scheduling or ticketing handoffs where available
  • Clear escalation paths and human review for sensitive actions
  • Tested integrations, defined support arrangements and ongoing improvement options

Not every chatbot is limited to FAQ answers, and no AI receptionist can independently guarantee every integration or outcome. The distinction is the independently scoped implementation, accountability and ongoing service.
